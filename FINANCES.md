# Cashbook guide

Use Finances for actual Cash, M-Pesa and Bank movements. Enter opening account balances before later transactions. Owner capital/drawings, loan principal, transfers and equipment purchases have distinct types and do not become operating income/expense.

Post actual receipts/payments from existing boat/fuel sources, confirming amount, account and date. Boat income is only a posting ceiling, not proof of the business's share. Use invoice, bill and payroll payment actions for those documents rather than repeating income/expense.

Cash surplus differs from boat net profit and from Accounting's accrual P&L. The cashbook trial balance covers financial postings; Accounting provides the broader statements with inventory, unpaid documents, earned wages and depreciation.

Use unique optional references. Review negative balances and statement differences rather than assuming automatic adjustments. Corrections retain originals. A posted source cannot be removed while active entries still depend on it.

All values are KSh. BUSINESS-GUIDE.md explains recognition methods, and SERVER.md explains optional secure staff mode. Automatic external backups, bank feeds and automatic M-Pesa connections are not included.

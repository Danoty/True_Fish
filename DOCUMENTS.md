# Branded reports and printing

Every printable business report includes the True Fish logo, company name, report title and print date. Receipts retain their own branded payment layout.

Business report downloads now produce self-contained **HTML documents** with the original PNG logo embedded in the file. This covers operations records, cashbooks, trial balances, profit and loss, balance sheets, daily summaries, fuel reconciliation, receipt registers and record history. Open the downloaded document in a browser, then choose **Print / Save as PDF** to produce a PDF or paper copy. The logo does not depend on internet access or an external image link.

Reports keep all exported rows and escape user-entered text. Wide operational records use stacked record sections instead of an unreadably wide table. Creating, downloading and printing a report do not post transactions.

The only data-file downloads are JSON restore backups and CSV import templates. These formats cannot carry visible images and remain machine-readable so restore and imports continue working. They are not branded business documents. Keep backups private.

The same branded reports work in the Android edition. Its file-saving screen recognises HTML reports, and native printing remains available for receipts and report pages.
